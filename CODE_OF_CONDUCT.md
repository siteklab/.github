# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct applies to every repository in the [siteklab](https://github.com/siteklab) organization, and to
issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space connected to them. It covers lab members and outside
contributors alike.

The lab's broader conduct expectations are in the [Conduct & Wellbeing](https://siteklab.github.io/handbook/chapters/conduct-wellbeing.html)
chapter of the lab handbook. This document applies them to our shared code and data.

## Our pledge

We want participation in our projects to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation. Good science needs people who notice different things and ask different questions.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels, including people who are new to git, code review or
  open science
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback on the work, not the person
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
- Crediting other people's ideas, code and data

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Sharing participant data or other protected information outside approved channels
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

Report concerns to Kevin Sitek at [kevin.sitek@utdallas.edu](mailto:kevin.sitek@utdallas.edu). Reports are handled as
confidentially as possible, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

If the concern involves Kevin, or you'd rather not go through him, the handbook's
[Conduct & Wellbeing](https://siteklab.github.io/handbook/chapters/conduct-wellbeing.html) chapter lists independent
contacts at UT Dallas, including the Title IX Coordinator and an anonymous Ethics and Compliance Hotline.
UT Dallas's Title IX and conduct policies apply as a baseline.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
code of conduct. Depending on the severity, responses range from a private conversation or written warning to a
temporary or permanent ban from the organization's repositories.
